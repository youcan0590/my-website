About Our Guide

Welcome to the Syrian Virtual University Events Guide, a platform designed to bring the university community together through events and activities.

Discover upcoming workshops, cultural festivals, sports activities, and other university events. Explore event details, find activities that match your interests, and stay connected with what's happening across SVU.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A connected virtual university where every student can easily find, follow, and take part in the events that matter to them, wherever they are.

Our Mission

To give the SVU community one clear place to discover events, share activities, and stay informed, so that culture, learning, and student life stay close together.

Policies & Standards

Event Submission Policy

Any student club or university branch can request to upload an event. The request must include: title, description, accurate date, and university location.

Content Standards

All events must align with the academic and cultural values of the Syrian Virtual University. Commercial advertisements or unrelated content will not be accepted.
